TWITTER

Twitter is a social media platform that allows users to post and interact with short messages known as "tweets." It is widely used for news, entertainment, and social networking.

INSTAGRAM

Instagram is a photo and video sharing social networking service that allows users to capture, edit, and share visual content with their followers. It is popular for its filters, stories, and reels.

YOUTUBE

YouTube is a video-sharing platform where users can upload, view, and share videos. It offers a wide range of content, including music videos, tutorials, vlogs, and live streams, making it one of the most popular.
